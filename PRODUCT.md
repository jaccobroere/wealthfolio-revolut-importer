# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Wealthfolio users importing a Revolut investment CSV into an existing account. They need a clear, low-friction first step that keeps the file local and makes the next review stages predictable.

## Product Purpose

This add-on converts a supported Revolut investment CSV into reviewed Wealthfolio activities. Its import flow protects data integrity through explicit mapping, reconciliation, and confirmation before anything is written.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, trustworthy, and familiar. The add-on should feel like a native Wealthfolio workflow rather than a separate broker-specific tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid bespoke upload controls, decorative marketing styling, hover-only interactions, and information that exposes statement contents or filenames.

## Design Principles

- Extend Wealthfolio's established component and state vocabulary.
- Make the next required action obvious without adding ceremony.
- Keep safety and privacy signals clear, concise, and local to the action.
- Preserve equivalent keyboard, pointer, and touch paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls with visible keyboard focus, keyboard activation, screen-reader labels and status updates, and touch-friendly targets. Do not rely on hover for functionality.
