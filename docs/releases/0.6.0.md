# Revolut Importer 0.6.0

## Highlights

- **The right listing, in your currency.** Revolut statements carry a ticker
  only, and the same ticker often exists on several exchanges — or belongs to
  an unrelated fund elsewhere. The importer now searches broadly (the ticker,
  its common exchange suffixes for your traded currency, and the instrument's
  name) and ranks listings: the same instrument first, then the currency you
  traded it in on Revolut, then your preferred exchanges.

- **Accept suggested listings.** One click accepts a listing only when it is
  the same instrument, in your traded currency, and strictly first on your
  exchange preference. Nothing is ever accepted without a reviewer action.

- **Search anything, pick anything.** Every ticker has its own search box (e.g.
  `IWDA.AS`, a fund name). Results show the exchange, MIC, currency and name,
  with badges for the suggestion and the traded currency. A resolved ticker
  can be changed with "Change".

- **Preferred exchanges per account.** Default: euro-area venues first, then
  the main US venues. Edit the order in the mapping step; it is saved for the
  account.

- **Forget remembered mappings.** One button (with confirmation) clears every
  mapping this importer remembers for the selected account. Other importers'
  mappings are untouched.

- Remembered mappings are re-verified against their own listing first, so a
  correct listing on another exchange is no longer reported as stale.

## Measured

On a private 21-ticker Revolut statement (15 traded in EUR, 6 in USD) against
a Wealthfolio 3.6.1 host: the plain ticker search's first result was in the
wrong currency for 7 tickers. The new ranking suggested a listing for all 21,
each in the currency it was traded in.

## Compatibility

- Wealthfolio: 3.6.1
- Add-on SDK: 3.6.1
